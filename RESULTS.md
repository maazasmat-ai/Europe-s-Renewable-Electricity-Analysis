# Results

Data: Our World in Data energy dataset, 2000–2025, 15 European countries/regions.

## Germany and the 2030 target

- Renewable share in 2025: **59.1 %**
- Trend since 2015: **+3.15 percentage points per year** → about **74.2 % in 2030**
- Needed to reach 80 % by 2030: **+4.18 pp per year**
- Note: the official target refers to gross electricity *consumption*; this analysis uses the share of *generation*, so it is an approximation.
